# Privacy Policy for TheaJump

**Effective date:** September 22, 2026

Robert Allan Bolista ("we," "us," or "our") operates the TheaJump mobile game (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App does — and does not — collect.

## Summary

TheaJump does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The App has no account creation, no network connection, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking of any kind.

## Information We Collect

**None.** TheaJump does not collect any personally identifiable information, device identifiers, location data, usage analytics, or any other data about you or your device.

## Local Data Storage

The App saves your high score and unlocked achievement badges directly on your device using standard local storage (iOS `UserDefaults` / Android `SharedPreferences`). This information:

- Never leaves your device
- Is never transmitted to us or any third party
- Is automatically deleted when you uninstall the App

## Sharing Your Score

TheaJump lets you optionally share your score using your device's built-in share sheet (the same system feature used by other apps). This only happens when you actively tap the "Share" button, and only sends the text you choose to whatever app or contact you pick — TheaJump itself does not collect or see this activity, and it is subject to the privacy policy of whichever app you share to.

## Third-Party Services

TheaJump does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or data collection SDKs. We do not share data with third parties because no data is collected.

## Children's Privacy

Because TheaJump collects no data of any kind, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the relevant age of digital consent in your region), and none is at risk of being collected.

## Your Rights

Since no personal data is collected or stored by us, there is no personal data to access, correct, export, or delete. Your high score, stored only on your own device, can be cleared at any time by uninstalling the App.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes in the future (for example, if new features require data collection), we will update this page and revise the effective date above.
